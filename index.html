Start to finish · The American burger

HOW IT'S
MADE.

  1. Part one · The patty
  2. 01

    Grind

    Chuck and brisket, 80/20, ground in-house every morning. Never frozen, barely touched.

  3. 02

    Roll

    Portioned to 120 g and rolled into a loose ball. Cold hands, no packing.

  4. 03

    Smash

    Pressed flat with the spatula for ten seconds on a 250°C griddle. Thin, wide, craggy.

  5. 04

    Sear & flip

    Ninety seconds until the edges go lacy and crisp, then one flip. Salt, pepper, nothing else.

  6. 05

    Melt

    Aged cheddar goes on the moment the patty turns, and melts into every crevice.

  7. Part two · The stack
  8. 06

    Toast & sauce

    Brioche bun toasted in beef fat, bottom half painted with house sauce.

  9. 07

    Crunch

    Cold crisp lettuce and dill pickles go on first, so the bun stays dry.

  10. 08

    The patty

    Straight off the iron onto the pickles, cheese still running.

  11. 09

    Onion & tomato

    Shaved red onion and a thick slice of ripe tomato.

  12. 10

    Crown

    The sesame-seeded top bun lands, and the burger is closed.

  13. 11

    Served

    To the table in under four minutes. Fries on the side.
